# Opportunity Universe

Revenue Automaton searches for money attached to unresolved problems, not only jobs.

## Discovery categories

- funded software/open-source bounties
- fixed-price freelance contracts and direct service requests
- QA, usability and browser/app testing
- AI evaluation, model review and data labeling
- paid research studies, interviews and consumer/expert panels
- paid voice, image, video and other consented data contribution
- transcription, captioning and translation
- responsible-disclosure / bug-bounty programs
- cash competitions, open-innovation challenges and prize contests
- grants, awards and procurement opportunities
- digital products and reusable service assets
- affiliate/referral programs with no required purchase or spend

## Evidence hierarchy

Search engines, social posts and aggregators are discovery leads. They are not authoritative proof.

Before economics, prefer the underlying buyer/sponsor/employer/program page and verify:
1. opportunity is still open;
2. reward is real cash or a deliberately accepted cash-equivalent;
3. no upfront spend, deposit, stake, purchase or paid subscription is required;
4. eligibility and device/location requirements;
5. payment can be independently verified;
6. whether the work requires the account owner personally;
7. competition/claim status when knowable.

Unknowns stay unknown. The system must not manufacture eligibility, availability, payment certainty or success probability.

## Scout and problem hypotheses

The GitHub Scout builds paid-search queries from the configured work capabilities. As results arrive during a run, it prioritizes capability topics that appeared in those results when choosing the next queries. This is bounded, in-run adaptation; it does not claim to retain a learning history across hourly runs.

Separate problem-signal searches can produce `UNVERIFIED_HYPOTHESIS` records from issues describing manual, repetitive, slow, error-prone or painful workflows. These records are leads for possible services or products, not paid opportunities. They do not enter candidate qualification, economics, ranking, transaction approval or realized P&L. A hypothesis can become an opportunity only after an authoritative buyer/payment path is independently verified through the normal gates.

## Priority

Large headline prizes do not automatically outrank small funded tasks. Probability-dependent rewards are marked speculative and should be evaluated separately from deterministic or funded work.

At zero realized capital, a modest verified task with no spend and a clear payment path can be preferable to a high-prize contest with uncertain selection.

## Human boundary

Research participation, interviews, identity checks, voice/video capture, screeners, attestations and other person-specific tasks are HUMAN_REQUIRED. Automation may discover, verify, prepare and calculate economics, but it must not impersonate the user or fabricate qualifying answers.

## VersionVault capability

VersionVault is an available zero-cost artifact-analysis capability for appropriate release-verification work. It remains a separate project. Revenue Automaton may account for that capability when estimating fulfillment feasibility/cost; it must not claim VersionVault performs live repository, CI, provenance or security-certification work that it does not perform.
