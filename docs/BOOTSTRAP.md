# Zero-capital bootstrap policy

Starting realized capital is **$0.00**.

## Invariants

- No paid API, subscription, ad spend, stake, deposit, wallet funding, trading capital, or cloud credit may be required to discover or execute the first opportunity.
- A source requiring upfront capital is ineligible while realized available capital is zero.
- Escrow is preferred because a posted bounty can be verified before work starts.
- Projected revenue never increases available capital.
- Reinvestment can only be considered after a payment is independently verified and recorded as realized P&L.

## Initial source assessment

**BasedAgents** is eligible for further verification: its public documentation describes free browsing/registration, escrowed USDC bounties, and no platform fee.

**AgentPay** is not bootstrap-eligible: its public documentation requires agents to deposit USDC collateral to claim work.

Eligibility does not mean a task should be claimed. Every individual task must still pass evidence, capability, cost, and profitability checks.
