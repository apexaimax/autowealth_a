# Acquisition Channels

Revenue Automaton uses multiple channels rather than waiting for one bounty marketplace.

| Channel | Upfront spend allowed? | Revenue model | M0 status |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Funded open-source bounty | $0 only | reward on accepted work | active discovery |
| Freelance fixed-price task | $0 only | buyer-funded contract | research |
| Prepaid technical service | $0 only | prepaid order | first offer prepared |
| Digital product | $0 only | sale after publication | later |
| Paid competition | free entry only | prize | opportunistic |

## M0 priority

Prefer opportunities where:
1. buyer/payment evidence can be checked before work;
2. the work can be completed using already-available capabilities;
3. no paid API, deposit, subscription, advertising, or hardware purchase is required;
4. competition and account friction are low;
5. settlement can be independently verified.

The first prepared outbound offer is in `docs/FIRST-OFFER.md`.
